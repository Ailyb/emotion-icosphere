# Claims checklist (PLAN_v2 standing rule; finish in T7)

Each line links to the file that holds the number. Wording rule: say "information beyond bag-of-words baselines", never "beyond topic or semantics" and never "internal state".

## Supported (three model families: Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct, Gemma-2-2B-it, Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3; all 4-bit, 2B-7B)
- Mistral-7B replicates the cross-domain result (probe beats TF-IDF trained on the same data on 3/3 external sources, +0.054 to +0.152 HUM-trained, +0.092 to +0.196 LLM-only; beats bag of embeddings on the frozen set +0.121 [+0.082, +0.160]); in-domain TF-IDF still wins (-0.083 [-0.125, -0.042]); the larger model does not close that gap. `data/eval_merged2/mlx-community__Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3-4bit/`.
- Replication (pre-registered criteria R1, R3 met on Gemma-2-2B): probe beats a bag-of-embeddings baseline on the frozen set (+0.112 [+0.073, +0.150]) and on all three external sources; probe beats TF-IDF cross-domain on 3/3 sources (+0.095 to +0.190 HUM-trained); in-domain TF-IDF still wins on GoEmotions (-0.085 [-0.128, -0.046]). Same direction and size as Llama. `data/eval_merged2/<model>/{frozen,external}_eval.md`.
- Probe-direction geometry correlates with valence-arousal-dominance distances between emotion words (NRC VAD v2.1) on TWO of three models: Llama rho +0.53 (lda, p=0.016) / +0.60 (meandiff, p=0.001); Gemma +0.61 (p=0.003) / +0.63 (p=0.001). On Mistral it holds for meandiff (+0.54, p=0.005) but not for the validation-selected logreg direction (+0.31, p=0.112), so the pre-registered rule is not met there. Exact permutation over 7! labelings; nominal, seven points. `t8_experiments.md`.
- Mean-pooling over tokens is a sound default: last-token and last-4 pooling are clearly worse (-0.05 to -0.15 macro-F1 on most sets), max-pool and multi-layer concatenation are equivalent (two models, Llama and Gemma, `variants.md`).
- A linear read-out is sufficient at the selected layer: an MLP and a valence-hierarchy do not beat flat logistic regression on >= 3 of 4 test sets (pre-registered rule). All three models. `t8_experiments.md`.
- Per-length recalibration of z-scores: with the existing calibration, 15-76% (Llama) / 20-83% (Gemma) / 15-69% (Mistral) of neutral human passages raise some |z| > 3 depending on length; after per-bucket cross-validated recalibration the alarm threshold for 5% false alarms is a uniform 2.9-3.3 (Llama), 2.9-3.1 (Gemma), 2.8-3.1 (Mistral) (descriptive, post hoc). `length_calibration.md`.
- Cross-domain transfer beats a lexical baseline (pre-registered H1, `external_eval.md`): trained on GoEmotions-derived data, the probe beats TF-IDF trained on the same data on dair-ai/emotion (+0.151 [+0.122, +0.178]), ISEAR (+0.132 [+0.101, +0.163]) and DailyDialog (+0.103 [+0.077, +0.130]); with LLM-only training the gaps are larger (+0.125 to +0.262, all CIs exclude 0). Also beats a bag-of-embeddings baseline on all three (+0.076 to +0.186). Holds on all three models. Not beyond a frozen sentence encoder trained on the same data (H3, see Not established). Caveats: dair labels hashtag-derived; ISEAR original terms are non-commercial research, CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 (read 2026-11-22; see NOTICE.md).
- Frozen human test set (GoEmotions official test, n=840, 8-way, hash-verified; `data/eval_merged2/mlx-community__Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct/frozen_eval.md`): probe beats a bag-of-embeddings baseline, +0.107 [+0.065, +0.148]; and beats block-0 states, +0.078 [+0.039, +0.116]. Wording: "information beyond a bag-of-embeddings baseline".
- Cross-writer transfer: probes trained only on LLM-written text score 0.305 [0.272, 0.338] on the frozen human set (shuffle-null p95 0.137, TF-IDF 0.146; probe − TF-IDF +0.159 [+0.122, +0.197]). Low in absolute terms. Same file.
- More human training data helps: native LR k=378 vs k=80 per class, +0.069 [+0.039, +0.099] (data-limited, not saturated). Same file.
- Once human training text is present, LLM-written training text adds no measurable gain (human-only vs LLM+human curves overlap). Same file.

## Not established
- (Post hoc) That one direction per emotion extracts what the hidden states hold: the trained classifier on the same states beats the frozen encoder in-domain (+0.071 to +0.091, CIs exclude 0) but not on ISEAR (-0.061 to -0.082), and the probe is below the trained classifier in-domain (-0.053 to -0.106). `<model>/t11_posthoc.md`.
- That the probes carry more emotion information than a generic frozen sentence encoder (T11 H3, pre-registered 0d74f80): NOT supported. Probe - all-mpnet-base-v2 (109.5M parameters; LR on top, trained on the same HUM data) has a CI above 0 on 0 of 3 external sources for 0 of 3 models; the encoder is better on ISEAR (-0.032 to -0.095) and DailyDialog (-0.038 to -0.087), tied on dair and on the frozen set. `data/eval_merged2/t11_summary.md`, `<model>/t11.md`.
- That a human-neutral threshold fixes neutral detection (T11 H5): NOT met (pre-registered run with the first-run probe; repeated post hoc with the HUM-trained probe: macro-F1 −0.171 / −0.163 / −0.127, `t11_h5_hum.md`). Held-out neutral recall rises from 0.14-0.31 to 0.90, but frozen 8-way macro-F1 falls by 0.121 / 0.173 / 0.134 (Llama / Gemma / Mistral; CIs exclude 0). `<model>/t11.md`.
- That the probe directions shift the emotion a model expresses, in general (steering): 0 of 7 on Llama-3.2-3B layer 16 (T10, pre-registered; claim needed >= 4) but 4 of 7 on Gemma-2-2B layer 14 (T12/C1 H7a, pre-registered; threshold pass, joy at 4 strengths and sadness, fear, surprise only at the largest fluent strength; 1 of 7 in the norm-matched regime alone). Model-dependent. That mean-difference directions beat probe directions at matched fluency: not established (H7b, 0/7). A same-data Gemma run (T14/C3, H9, pre-registered f4cabfe) also found no advantage: 0 of 7 paired intervals above 0 (`steering_c3_results.md`); the Llama contrast stays confounded with the training corpus. `steering_results.md`, `steering_c1_results.md`. Llama was re-run at fluency-matched strengths (T13/C1b, pre-registered): probe directions again 0 of 7.
- That prompting or another pooling gives a better representation: pre-registered rule (>= 3 of 4 sets) met by no variant on either model; the Llama prompted read-out gained on 2 of 4 sets (ISEAR +0.136, DailyDialog +0.072) but did not replicate on Gemma (0 of 4). `variants.md`.
- That the probe beats TF-IDF in-domain on human text: it does NOT on the GoEmotions frozen set (same domain as its human training data); TF-IDF is better, −0.088 [−0.128, −0.046] (k=378: −0.090 [−0.126, −0.053]), and adding hidden states to TF-IDF gives +0.018 [−0.018, +0.053] (fusion, post hoc). The earlier "probe beats TF-IDF" result was on LLM-written and hand-written (non-blind) text; the cross-domain result above is the clean version of that claim.
- Probe vs trained classifier on the same states: +0.044 [-0.045, +0.129] on the hand-written set (CI includes 0); weaker on TEST-all (-0.065 [-0.105, -0.026]) and TEST-hard (-0.055 [-0.117, +0.005]). Pre-merge it was worse (-0.100 [-0.186, -0.010]). Why it changed is not isolated (no ablation). `report_v3.md`, `docs/FINDINGS.md`.
- Short-text reliability: only 1 short own-voice passage; untested. `v3/confusion_v3.md`.
- Calm: appears only in LLM-written text (the human corpus has no calm), so calm results are writer-confounded.
- Qwen-written corpus is lexically harder than v1 but not clearly hard: TF-IDF 8-way macro-F1 0.708 [0.678, 0.741] (gate was < 0.7). Command: `python probekit_tfidf_gate.py --corpus data/corpus/corpus_v3_qwen3.8-27b.jsonl`.

## Rejected
- "The probe's z-scores are calibrated (|z| > 3 is rare on neutral text)": false at every length with the existing calibration; after per-bucket recalibration still fails the pre-registered per-passage bound (<= 5% upper) in every bucket because tails are heavier than Gaussian. The 1-2 word bucket also lacks n >= 500.
- "The probe beats a lexical baseline on human text" (frozen set, equal training data: it does not).
- "LLM-written corpora are sufficient for probes that work on human text" (0.305 with no human rows vs 0.51+ with them).
- Any claim that the probes read the model's "internal emotional state" or feelings.
- The v1 result (acc 0.860) as evidence of anything: lexical TF-IDF scored macro-F1 0.931 [0.906, 0.953] on that corpus (`data/corpus/corpus_v1_tfidf_gate.md`). `docs/FINDINGS.md`.
- "Short-text z-scores are valid": the neutral short sentence "The sky is blue." scored sadness z +4.9 (anecdote that motivates T3).
- Writer-shift-free: leave-one-writer-out with reddit held out gives F1 0.235-0.284. `robustness_*/robustness.md` under `data/eval_merged/...`.

## Visualization pages (viz_plan Option 1; 2026-11-26; copy reviewed against this file)
- Supported (one model: Llama-3.2-3B, 4-bit, layer 16, HUM-trained probe; model-written synthetic text only): on 1,559 synthetic passages (`data/viz/synth.jsonl`, writer granite4.2:8b, intended labels unvalidated), the probe with the per-length recalibrated 95th-percentile threshold labels 0-2% of neutral passages as an emotion in every length bucket (120 per bucket) and misses 66-71% of 1-4 word emotional passages and 19-22% of passages of 12+ words. Source: `docs/viz/synth_summary.json`. Not a claim about human text: model-written neutral text is easier, and transfer from model-written to human text is low (0.305; see above).
- The threshold is descriptive and post hoc (out-of-fold 95th percentile of max|z| on human neutral text); it is not a calibration. The per-passage bound is still rejected in every bucket (see Rejected), so the pages say so and mark the shortest buckets (observed recalibrated false-alarm rate above 5%) as low confidence.
- The baselines on the page (TF-IDF, native LR, encoder) have no false-alarm control, so hit rates are not like for like; the page says so.
- Wording the pages must keep: "emotion-concept probe" read-outs, never "feels" or "internal state"; TF-IDF scored higher in-domain on GoEmotions; steering is model-dependent (0/7 Llama, 4/7 Gemma, Mistral untested). Live mode scores typed text in memory only, on 127.0.0.1; it never stores or logs it.
- Hand-written, own-voice and external-test text are never shown; all example passages are new synthetic text written for the demo.
- Option 2/3 pages (2026-11-27): token-level stream colour passed its pre-registered splice rule on synthetic text for Llama-3.2-3B only (T15: hit 0.700 [0.621, 0.786], onset 8 tokens, offset 2, neutral false alarms 1.8%); hit rates differ by emotion (surprise 0.30, sadness 0.45, disgust 0.95), intended labels are unvalidated, and warm-up positions can flag early. Steering-lab labels come from a judge whose consistency on generated text is below the pre-registered bar (T16: kappa 0.556 Gemma, 0.642 Llama; `judge_gen.md`), so labels on steered text are NOT validated; any page showing them must say so (the lab pages do).
