Steering lab (static; local models only)

Results are model-dependent: LDA probe directions steered 0 of 7 emotions on Llama-3.2-3B and 4 of 7 on Gemma-2-2B (a threshold pass at the largest fluent strength). Mean-difference directions were not shown to beat them at matched fluency (a same-data comparison on Gemma found no advantage: 0 of 7 paired intervals above 0). Random directions are drawn so you can judge for yourself. This shows changes in expressed emotion in short neutral texts, not an isolated emotion mechanism and not a change in decisions.

Steering = adding a direction (scaled by the mean token-state norm) to the residual stream while a model continues a neutral prompt. These numbers are from pre-registered runs (40 prompts, 20 random directions each); the lab only displays them. It is not a way to manipulate people, and it is not offered as a service.
