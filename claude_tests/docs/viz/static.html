Reading the probe honestly

Static scores for synthetic example passages (mlx-community/Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct, layer 16, lda probe trained on the paper's primary HUM set; n = 4192 training passages).

What this is not: a reading of what the model feels. These are linear read-outs of emotion-concept text patterns, and the paper finds a TF-IDF classifier often does as well in-domain. Passages are new text written for this demo and were not used to tune anything. Grey means below the false-alarm threshold for that length. Grey is the default.

Summary on a larger synthetic set

Passages written by a local language model (not the probe model), about 120 neutral and 20 per emotion in each length bucket. Intended labels are the writer's request and were not validated, so hit rates are approximate. 95% Clopper-Pearson intervals. Two cautions: (1) only the probe has a threshold set for a false-alarm rate (5% on human neutral text); the baselines pick the most likely label with no such control, so their hit rates are not like for like. (2) Model-written neutral text (schedules, facts) is easier than human neutral text, so the probe's false-alarm rate here is lower than the 5% it targets.

How to read this

  • Threshold. A passage is called emotional only if its largest recalibrated z exceeds the out-of-fold 95th percentile of max|z| on human neutral text of the same length (so about 5% of neutral human text is flagged by construction). Rule fixed before scoring these passages.
  • Short text. A bucket is marked low confidence when the observed recalibrated false-alarm rate on human neutral text exceeds 5% (the shortest buckets). The paper's strict upper-bound criterion fails in every bucket, so even unmarked rows are approximate.
  • Baselines. TF-IDF, native-state LR and a frozen sentence encoder are trained on the same data as the probe. Where they disagree with the probe, no one of them is ground truth.
  • Bundled neutral passages shown as emotional (small n, descriptive, by length bucket): 1-2: 0/1; 12-19: 0/4; 20+: 0/2; 5-7: 0/1; 8-11: 0/3. This is not the acceptance test; the reserved human neutral set in length_calibration.md is.
  • Steering is a separate question. Reading emotion text is not writing it: probe directions steered 0 of 7 emotions on Llama-3.2-3B and 4 of 7 (threshold pass) on Gemma-2-2B.
