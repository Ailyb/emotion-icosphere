- **Class coverage.** Any class missing from a writer is writer-confounded. (i) vs (ii): paired bootstrap difference of Delta_e over prompts, reported per emotion, no claim rule. Comparisons: 7 emotions x 3 strengths = 21 specificity tests, unadjusted; the >= 4-of-7 rule is the guard. Off-target effects (steering with e on other labels) are reported as a matrix, descriptively.
